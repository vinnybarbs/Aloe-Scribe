Aloe Scribe vs Granola

Granola is the closest product to Aloe Scribe in spirit. Both are notepads first, both capture the audio your computer already plays and hears, and neither puts a bot in your call. The difference is where the work happens. Granola captures audio on your machine and streams it to cloud services for transcription and AI notes. Aloe Scribe does the whole job on your computer. If you want polished cloud AI, team sharing, and mobile apps, Granola is the well-funded leader. If your meetings cannot leave the machine, that is what Aloe Scribe is for.

The real difference is where the processing happens

Granola got the form factor right, and we say that as fans. A quiet notepad that enhances your own typed notes is the best interaction design in this category, and Aloe Scribe's notepad comes from the same school. Where the two products part ways is architecture. Granola records locally but processes in the cloud. Its security page says meeting audio goes to transcription providers such as Deepgram and Assembly, notes are written by AI providers such as OpenAI and Anthropic, and the resulting transcripts are stored on Granola's servers. Granola does not keep the audio itself, but your meeting content leaves your machine by design, because that is how the product works.

That design now has legal weight on it. A putative class action, Chamberlain v. Granola, was filed in the Northern District of California in July 2026, alleging that Granola records participants without their consent and uses transcripts to train its models by default, with the opt-out belonging to the account holder rather than the people recorded. See the National Law Review's analysis and this case summary. These are allegations, not findings, and the case may go nowhere. But the exposure only exists because a vendor holds copies of the meetings.

Aloe Scribe removes the vendor. Recording, transcription, speaker identification, and the summary all run on your computer. There is no transcription service to subpoena, no training default to audit, and no server to breach. The only network calls the app makes are model downloads and update checks.

Choose Granola if

  • You want the most polished notepad in the category. Granola defined this product shape, and the cloud models writing its notes are excellent.
  • Your team shares notes. Shared folders, templates, and integrations with Notion, Slack, HubSpot, and Attio are real team features Aloe Scribe does not have.
  • You take meetings on your phone. Granola has iOS and Android apps. Aloe Scribe is a desktop app.
  • You want calendar integration that lines your notes up with your schedule.
  • Your organization has reviewed cloud processing and is comfortable with audio going to Granola's transcription and AI providers.

Choose Aloe Scribe if

  • Your meetings are confidential. Client calls, legal, HR, security, or anything under NDA. When nothing leaves the machine, there is nothing for a vendor to hold, train on, or produce in discovery.
  • You want speaker names that come from the audio itself, in any meeting app, not just the platforms with an integration.
  • You want your notes as plain files you own. Open Markdown on your disk cannot be locked away from your own scripts and tools.
  • You work offline or on networks where streaming meeting audio out is not an option.
  • You would rather not pay per seat. Aloe Scribe is free while in beta, with no account.

Try it on your next meeting

Download takes a few minutes, and the first transcript lands seconds after you hit stop. There is no account to create and nothing to cancel if it is not for you.
